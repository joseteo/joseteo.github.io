Dream Car Garage: Founder, Designer & Sole Engineer
A production car-collection web app I conceived, designed, built, deployed, and now maintain entirely on my own. Car enthusiasts assemble their dream garage from 600+ vehicles, or let an in-house AI Build Generator curate one from a natural-language description, all processed on EU-sovereign infrastructure with zero data retention. Users track how their collection evolves, compete on global leaderboards, and explore Carpedia, a built-in automotive encyclopedia. Eight-language search, multi-currency support, a five-phase roadmap through 2028, and full GDPR and EU AI Act compliance. It's live, it's growing, and I own every layer of it.
The Problem & The Vision
Car enthusiasts spend hours imagining the collection they'd own if money were no object, but that passion lives in scattered tabs, spreadsheets, and forum posts. There was no single, beautiful place to build, refine, and share a dream garage, and to feel it grow over time. Dream Car Garage exists to give the car community that home.
The product is built around a simple, addictive loop: browse a curated catalog of the world's most desirable cars, add them to a limited set of garage bays that force real trade-offs, then snapshot your collection as it evolves and measure it against everyone else. It's part fantasy tool, part social game, part reference library.
Where It Is Today: A Live, Maintained Product
Dream Car Garage is live at dreamcargarage.app as a fully functional product, not a prototype. The current release includes:
AI Build Generator. Users describe their dream garage in natural language (budget, era, driving style, region) and an in-house AI drafts a brief, then sources real matching vehicles from the 600-car catalog. No hallucinated cars, no invented specs: every result is a real vehicle in the collection. The AI runs entirely on self-hosted EU servers with zero data retention.
Garage builder. Users fill a limited set of bays from a catalog of 600+ vehicles across 11 categories (hypercars, supercars, GTs, hot hatches, SUVs, luxury and more), with adjustable garage size that creates real curation trade-offs.
Browse & discovery. A searchable, filterable, sortable collection with category tabs and brand filters spanning 74 manufacturers, from Ferrari and Bugatti to Caterham and Gordon Murray, with natural-language search in 8 languages.
Evolution. Collections are snapshotted into stages, so users can see how their taste and garage change over time and track swaps between snapshots.
Leaderboard & scoring. A "Share & Score" system and global leaderboard with category-specific rankings (Hypercars, JDM, Track Day, GTs, Hot Hatches, and more) turn private collections into a social, competitive experience.
Carpedia & Brands. A built-in automotive encyclopedia with editorial content: budget guides, category rankings, brand histories, and deep dives (from V12 engines to car culture), giving the platform reference-library depth alongside the curation game.
Accounts & saved garages. Users sign in to persist multiple named garages, duplicate them, and switch between profiles.
Internationalization. Full multi-language support with natural-language search in 8 languages and multi-currency pricing (GBP, EUR, USD).
Monetization. Sponsored placements integrated natively into the collection feed, plus an email subscription channel for retention.
EU data sovereignty & compliance. All AI processing runs on self-hosted European servers with no data leaving the EU. Privacy by Design with hashed IPs, encrypted tokens, consent-based analytics, and GDPR compliance. Complete legal layer including legal notice, privacy policy, cookie policy with consent manager, terms of use, and trademark attribution for every manufacturer. Built with the EU AI Act framework in mind.
What "Solo Founder" Actually Means Here
There is no team. Every role a startup normally distributes across people, I hold at once:
Product & design. Ideation, UX, visual design, and the entire interaction model are mine, with deliberate attention to detail at every step.
Full-stack engineering. The application is built on Next.js and React, with user authentication, persistent data, a 600+ vehicle catalog, search/filter/sort systems, the leaderboard and evolution mechanics, an image pipeline for car assets, and an in-house AI engine running on EU-sovereign infrastructure.
Deployment & operations. I deploy, monitor, and keep the service online. The hard, unglamorous part of a startup isn't shipping the demo, it's maintaining a production system that a community relies on every day.
Troubleshooting & maintenance. When something breaks, there is no one else. Diagnosing issues in production, fixing them, and shipping the fix is entirely on me.
Growth, SEO & monetization. Discoverability, ad integration, and the retention loop are mine to design and tune.
Legal, compliance & AI governance. Privacy, cookies, terms, trademark handling, GDPR compliance, and EU AI Act alignment were all researched and implemented by me.
Carrying a community on your back means the standard isn't "it works on my machine." It's "it works for everyone, all the time." That responsibility shapes every decision.
Engineering Highlights
In-house AI infrastructure. Designed and deployed a natural-language AI Build Generator on self-hosted EU servers: prompt intake, brief generation, constraint-aware vehicle matching, and structured output, all with zero data retention and full EU data sovereignty.
Data at scale. Modeling and maintaining 600+ vehicles across 11 categories and 74 brands, each with specs, imagery, pricing, and classification, kept consistent and fast to query.
Performance. Server-rendered pages and an optimized image pipeline keep a media-heavy catalog responsive, loading a paginated subset of the full collection on demand.
Stateful, persistent UX. Saved garages, evolution snapshots, and per-profile state require careful client/server data modeling and authentication.
Internationalization done properly. Natural-language search in 8 languages, multi-currency pricing, and full UI localization, not an afterthought bolted on at the end.
Where It's Headed: The Roadmap
Dream Car Garage follows a five-phase roadmap through 2028, each phase named after a Greek letter and building on the last:
Alpha: The Foundation (live now). 600-car catalog, Garage Builder, EU-sovereign AI Build Generator, leaderboards, evolution journeys, accounts, and 8-language natural-language search.
Beta: The Daily Habit (months 1-6). Living garages with depreciation and reliability alerts, deep car customization, garage statistics, and shareable stat cards.
Gamma: The Simulation Layer (months 6-14). Ownership cost simulator, model intelligence database, and viral verdict features.
Delta: The Social Graph (year 2). Public garage profiles, garage battles, car clubs, community rankings, and native iOS/Android companion apps.
Epsilon: Intelligence & Real-World Layer (year 2-3). Conversational Garage AI Assistant, AI garage rating system, real ownership sync, and market intelligence.
The ambition is to become an operating system for the car community: "If a feature makes the gap between a dream and reality feel smaller, build it."
